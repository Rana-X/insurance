Section III · What We Do Not Cover

Section IIIWhat We Do Not Cover

In shortPart A applies to every coverage. Part B adds a few exclusions that apply only to specific coverages. Many exclusions have exceptions (“This exclusion does not apply to…”) that restore coverage in specific situations.

A. Exclusions that apply to all coverages

We will not pay Loss, or provide services, based on, arising from or attributable to any of the following:

1.Bodily injury and property damage. Bodily Injury, or physical damage to or destruction of tangible property, including loss of use of that property. This exclusion does not apply to (a) mental anguish or emotional distress alleged in a Claim for a Privacy Wrongful Act or Media Wrongful Act; or (b) Hardware covered under Coverage N. Data is not tangible property.

2.Prior knowledge and prior notice. Any Incident, Wrongful Act or circumstance that (a) an Executive knew about before the Continuity Date and reasonably could have expected to lead to Loss or a Claim; or (b) was reported under any policy that was in force before this one.

3.Prior or pending proceedings. Any lawsuit, Regulatory Proceeding or other proceeding against an Insured that was pending before the Continuity Date, or any later Claim based on the same or Related facts.

4.Intentional and dishonest acts. Any dishonest, fraudulent, criminal or malicious act, or knowing violation of law, committed by an Executive or with an Executive's knowledge or consent. This exclusion applies only if a final, non-appealable judgment or ruling, or the Insured's own admission, establishes the conduct. We will defend the Claim until then. The conduct of one Insured is not imputed to any other natural person Insured. This exclusion does not apply to the acts of employees who are not Executives, if no Executive knew of or consented to them.

5.War and state cyber operations.

(a)War; or

(b)a Cyber Operation that has a major detrimental impact on the functioning of a State by significantly impairing that State's ability to provide Essential Services, or its security or defense.

Part (b) does not apply to your Computer System if it is not physically located in the impacted State. In deciding whether a Cyber Operation was carried out by, or at the direction of, a State, we will consider first whether the government of the State where your Computer System is located has attributed it to another State. Until any attribution is made, we may rely on an objectively reasonable inference from the available evidence. We have the burden of proving this exclusion applies. An act of terrorism, as defined in the Terrorism Risk Insurance Act, is not War. For this exclusion:

(i)“War” means the use of physical force by a State against another State, or as part of a civil war, rebellion, revolution, insurrection or military or usurped power, whether or not war is declared;

(ii)“Cyber Operation” means the use of a computer system by, or at the direction of, a State to disrupt, deny, degrade, manipulate or destroy information in a computer system of or in another State;

(iii)“State” means a sovereign state; and

(iv)“Essential Services” means services essential to the maintenance of vital functions of a State, including financial institutions and financial market infrastructure, health services and utility services.

6.Infrastructure failure. Any failure, interruption or outage of electricity, gas, water, internet backbone, telecommunications, satellite or other public infrastructure not under an Insured's operational control. This exclusion does not apply to a Security Failure of your own Computer System. A Dependent Business is not public infrastructure.

7.Natural perils. Fire, smoke, explosion, lightning, wind, water, flood, earthquake, volcanic eruption, landslide, hail or any other natural peril. This exclusion does not apply to a Privacy Event involving lost or stolen devices or records, or to the loss or theft of Hardware described in Definition 59(d).

8.Pollution and nuclear. The actual, alleged or threatened release of pollutants of any kind, or nuclear reaction, radiation or radioactive contamination.

9.Government seizure. The seizure, confiscation, nationalization or destruction of your Computer System or Data by order of a government. This exclusion does not apply to a Regulatory Proceeding under Coverage J.

10.Contractual liability. Liability an Insured assumed under a contract, warranty or guarantee. This exclusion does not apply to (a) liability the Insured would have had without the contract; (b) an obligation in a contract to protect the security or confidentiality of Personal Information or Confidential Information; or (c) PCI Fines and Assessments under Coverage K.

11.Professional services. An act, error or omission in rendering, or failing to render, professional services to others (for example accounting, tax, legal, financial, consulting or technology advice or work). This exclusion does not apply to Loss under the first-party coverages, or to the extent a Claim alleges a Security Wrongful Act or Privacy Wrongful Act.

12.Securities, antitrust and unfair trade. Any actual or alleged violation of securities laws; antitrust, price-fixing or restraint of trade laws; or laws against unfair or deceptive trade practices. The last part does not apply to a Claim or Regulatory Proceeding arising from a Security Failure or Privacy Event.

13.Employment practices and benefit plans. Employment-related practices, including wrongful termination, discrimination and harassment, or the administration of any employee benefit plan. This exclusion does not apply to a Claim arising from a Privacy Event involving the Personal Information of employees or plan participants.

14.Intellectual property. Infringement or misappropriation of any patent or trade secret, or infringement of any other intellectual property right. This exclusion does not apply to (a) a Media Wrongful Act other than patent infringement or trade secret misappropriation; or (b) the disclosure of a third party's trade secret that is Confidential Information because of a Security Failure or Privacy Event.

15.Wrongful collection and tracking.

(a)The collection, capture, storage or use of biometric information (such as fingerprints, voiceprints, or scans of the face, hand, retina or iris) in violation of any law, including the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act; or

(b)a Tracking Wrongful Act, unless Coverage Q applies.

This exclusion does not apply to a Privacy Event caused by a Security Failure, such as the theft of stored biometric data by a hacker.

16.Unsolicited communications. Any actual or alleged violation of the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, the CAN-SPAM Act, or any other law regulating telemarketing, unsolicited calls, texts, faxes or emails. This exclusion does not apply if the communications were sent by a third party through unauthorized access to your Computer System.

17.Insured versus Insured. A Claim brought by or on behalf of one Insured against another. This exclusion does not apply to a Claim by an employee arising from a Privacy Event involving that employee's Personal Information, or to a Claim by an additional insured described in Definition 31(d).

18.Money, securities and trading. The loss, theft, or reduction in value of Money, Securities, digital currency or other financial assets of an Insured, or any trading loss. This exclusion does not apply to an Extortion Payment under Coverage C or Direct Financial Loss under Coverage H.

19.Betterment and ordinary business costs. The cost of upgrading, improving or replacing any system beyond its condition before the Incident, except as covered under Coverage G or N; or your ordinary operating costs, salaries and overhead, except as part of Income Loss or Extra Expense.

20.Value of data and intellectual property. The monetary value of Data, trade secrets or other intellectual property of an Insured, including the cost of the research or development used to create it.

B. Exclusions that apply to specific coverages

1.Business interruption (Coverages D, E and M). We will not pay for:

(a)loss caused by unfavorable business conditions, loss of market, or any other loss beyond the Period of Restoration;

(b)an Interruption caused by planned or scheduled downtime or maintenance, or by ordinary wear and tear;

(c)contractual penalties, service credits or liquidated damages for failing to perform a contract;

(d)legal costs or Claim-related amounts, which are covered, if at all, only under the liability coverages; or

(e)under Coverages D and M, an Interruption of a Dependent Business's computer systems, which is covered, if at all, only under Coverage E.

2.Fraud (Coverage H). We will not pay for:

(a)any loss caused by a dishonest or fraudulent act of an Insured, whether acting alone or in collusion with others (employee theft is a matter for a crime or fidelity policy);

(b)any payment for goods, services or investments that were genuinely provided or offered, even if they turned out to be worth less than expected or were misrepresented by the seller;

(c)any loss arising from credit you extended, or a customer's failure to pay, except as covered for Invoice Manipulation; or

(d)any amount you recover from your financial institution, the recipient or any other source.

3.Media (Coverage L). We will not pay for a Claim arising from (a) false, misleading or comparative advertising about the price or quality of your own goods or services; (b) the failure of goods or services to conform to their advertised quality or performance; or (c) a dispute with an independent contractor or former employee over ownership of Media Content they created for you.

Section IV · Limits, Retention and Coinsurance

Section IVLimits, Retention and Coinsurance

In shortWe pay up to $1,000,000 in total. You pay one retention per incident. The first 72 hours of incident response are free. Keeping the security controls you told us about keeps your best terms.

A. Policy Aggregate Limit

The Policy Aggregate Limit in Item 4 of the Declarations is the most we will pay for all Loss under all coverages combined, including Claim Expenses, for the Policy Period and any Extended Reporting Period. When it is used up, we have no further obligation to pay Loss or to defend any Claim.

B. Coverage limits

The limit shown for each coverage in Item 6 is the most we will pay for all Loss under that coverage. Each coverage limit is part of, and not in addition to, the Policy Aggregate Limit, even where the two amounts are the same. Payments under a coverage reduce both its limit and the Policy Aggregate Limit.

C. Incident Response Services

Incident Response Services under Coverage A do not reduce any limit, and no Retention applies to them.

D. Retention

1.You must pay the Retention for each Incident or Claim before we pay Loss. Claim Expenses count toward the Retention. We may pay Loss within the Retention and ask you to repay us.

2.One retention per incident. If a single Incident, including any Claims arising from it, triggers more than one coverage, you pay only the largest single Retention that applies.

3.No Retention applies to Coverage A or Coverage G. For Coverages D, E and M, the Waiting Period applies instead of a dollar Retention.

4.For Coverage H, if Coverage H, paragraph 4, is met, the Retention is half of the amount that would otherwise apply to that Incident (for example, $2,500 instead of $5,000). If the same Incident also triggers another coverage, paragraph 2 applies.

E. Waiting Period

For Coverages D, E and M, we pay only if the Interruption lasts longer than the Waiting Period, and we do not pay Business Interruption Loss incurred during the Waiting Period. If an Interruption stops and then restarts from the same cause within 72 hours, we treat it as one continuous Interruption.

F. Related Incidents and Claims

Related Incidents and Related Claims are treated as a single Incident or Claim. One Retention applies, and only the limits of the policy in force when the first of them was Discovered or made apply, even if some occur after this policy ends.

G. Security credit adjustment

1.Item 7 of the Declarations lists security credits you earned, based on the controls described in your Application. If one of those controls was not in place when an Incident began, we will adjust the terms for that Incident only, as if that credit had not been earned:

(a)MFA and endpoint detection and response: the Retention for that Incident becomes the standard retention in Item 5;

(b)backups: the ransomware coinsurance in Section IV.H applies; and

(c)callback verification: the Coverage H limit for that Incident becomes $100,000.

2.We will not make this adjustment if (a) the control was not in place for less than 30 days because of a change you were actively fixing; or (b) the missing control did not contribute to the Incident or increase the Loss.

3.This adjustment replaces any right we might otherwise have to deny coverage, or to rescind or cancel this policy, because a control was not in place. It does not limit our rights under Section VI.A if the Application contained a misrepresentation made with intent to deceive.

H. Ransomware coinsurance

If Item 7 shows that the backup credit was not earned, or Section IV.G applies to it, you will bear 20% of all Loss arising from a Ransomware Event after the Retention, and we will pay 80%. Our share is subject to the applicable limits. Coinsurance does not apply to Incident Response Services.

I. Allocation

If a Claim or Incident involves both covered and uncovered matters or parties, we will pay 100% of Claim Expenses until the covered matters are resolved. For all other Loss, you and we will each use best efforts to agree on a fair allocation based on the relative legal and financial exposures.

Section V · When Something Happens

Section VWhen Something Happens

In shortCall us as soon as you can. Use our panel for the fastest response. Make legal notices on time. Don't pay ransoms, settle or admit fault without us.

A. Reporting Incidents and Claims

1.You must report every Incident and Claim to us through the Hotline as soon as practicable after an Executive first Discovers the Incident or receives the Claim.

2.Claims must be reported during the Policy Period, or no later than 60 days after it ends (or during any Extended Reporting Period).

3.Incidents under the first-party coverages must be reported no later than 60 days after the Policy Period ends. We will not deny coverage for an Incident because it was reported late within that window unless the delay materially harmed our ability to investigate or reduce the Loss, and then only to the extent of that harm.

4.Your report should include what happened, when it was Discovered, the systems and information involved, and copies of any Claim, demand or notice received.

B. Notice of circumstances

If, during the Policy Period, you become aware of circumstances that could reasonably lead to a Claim, and you give us written notice describing them (including the Wrongful Act, the people involved and why a Claim is expected), any later Claim arising from those circumstances will be treated as first made on the date of your notice.

C. Panel Providers and your choice of providers

1.You may use any Panel Provider without asking us for further consent. Their rates are pre-agreed with us.

2.You may ask to use a provider that is not on our panel. We will not unreasonably withhold consent. We will pay that provider's reasonable fees up to the amount we would have paid a Panel Provider for comparable services.

3.If you incur reasonable costs to contain an Incident in an emergency before you can reach us, we will treat them as if we had consented, provided you report the Incident within 48 hours after incurring them.

D. Legal notification deadlines

You never need our consent to make a notification that a law or regulation requires, or to meet a legal deadline. We will not deny or reduce coverage because you made a legally required notification without our consent.

E. Your other duties after an Incident or Claim

Every Insured must:

(a)take reasonable steps to contain the Incident and reduce Loss;

(b)preserve evidence, including logs, affected devices, emails and ransom notes, and not wipe or rebuild affected systems until our forensic provider agrees;

(c)cooperate with us and our Panel Providers, give us access to relevant systems, records and people, and help us enforce any rights against others;

(d)not admit liability, make settlement offers, agree to pay any amount, or incur Claim Expenses without our prior consent, except as allowed in Section V.D; and

(e)tell us promptly about any recovery or payment received from another source.

F. Extortion demands

Before any Extortion Payment is made:

(a)you must obtain our written consent, and must not pay, promise to pay or negotiate a payment without it;

(b)you must, where we reasonably ask, report the threat to the Federal Bureau of Investigation or another appropriate law enforcement agency;

(c)you must allow us, or a Panel Provider, to check that the payment does not involve a person, organization or country subject to economic sanctions (see Section VI.K); and

(d)you must take reasonable steps to confirm that the threat is credible and that restoring from backups is not a reasonable alternative.

We may pay the Extortion Payment directly or reimburse you. Paying an Extortion Payment is always your decision. We will never require you to make one.

G. Fraudulent payments

If you Discover Funds Transfer Fraud, Social Engineering Fraud or Invoice Manipulation, you must, as soon as possible, (a) ask your financial institution to stop, recall or reverse the transfer; (b) file a complaint with the FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (ic3.gov) or other law enforcement; and (c) report it to us through the Hotline. Within 90 days after Discovery, you must send us a signed proof of loss with supporting records.

H. Defense and settlement

1.We have the right and duty to defend any Claim covered under Coverages I, J, K, L and Q, even if its allegations are groundless or false. We will appoint defense counsel, normally a Panel Provider. We will consider in good faith any counsel you propose.

2.We will not settle a Claim without your consent. If you refuse a settlement that we recommend and that the claimant is willing to accept, the most we will pay for that Claim is the amount of the recommended settlement, plus Claim Expenses incurred up to the date you refused, plus 70% of any Loss after that date. You will bear the remaining 30%.

3.You may settle any Claim for a total amount within the Retention, with our consent, which we will not unreasonably withhold.

I. Business interruption: proving and calculating your loss

1.You must send us a proof of loss within 180 days after the Period of Restoration ends. We will extend this deadline if you ask before it passes.

2.We will calculate Income Loss using your net profit and operating expenses in the 12 months before the Interruption, adjusted for seasonal patterns, trends, and the business you would reasonably have done during the Period of Restoration. We will take into account any business you make up after the Interruption ends, and any expenses you save because of it.

3.You must use reasonable efforts to resume operations, including by using other systems, locations or providers, and to reduce the Loss.

J. Appraisal

If you and we disagree only on the amount of Business Interruption Loss, Restoration Costs or Hardware replacement costs, either may demand an appraisal in writing. Each will choose an independent appraiser within 20 days. The appraisers will choose an umpire. A decision agreed by any two of the three is binding on the amount only. Each party pays its own appraiser and half of the umpire's fees.

K. Payment

We will pay covered Loss within 30 days after we receive the documents we reasonably need to verify it, or after an agreement, judgment or appraisal award fixes the amount. Where it is clear that part of a first-party Loss is covered, we will pay that part without waiting for the rest to be settled.

Section VI · General Conditions

Section VIGeneral Conditions

A. The Application

We issued this policy relying on the Application, which is part of this policy. The knowledge of one natural person Insured is not imputed to any other natural person Insured. Only the knowledge of the person who signed the Application and of Executives is imputed to the Insured Organization. We may rescind this policy only if the Application contained a misrepresentation that was made with intent to deceive and that materially affected our decision to issue the policy or its terms. Inaccurate statements about security controls that were not made with intent to deceive are dealt with only under Section IV.G.

B. The Named Insured acts for all Insureds

The Named Insured acts on behalf of every Insured to give and receive notices, pay premiums, receive return premiums, agree to changes, and buy an Extended Reporting Period.

C. Cancellation

1.You may cancel this policy at any time by notifying us. We will refund the unearned premium on a pro rata basis.

2.We may cancel this policy only (a) for non-payment of premium, by giving you at least 10 days' written notice; or (b) for fraud or a material misrepresentation described in Section VI.A, by giving you at least 30 days' written notice. We will refund any unearned premium pro rata.

D. Extended Reporting Period

1.Automatic. If this policy is cancelled or not renewed for any reason other than non-payment of premium or fraud, you automatically have 60 days after the Policy Period ends to report Claims first made during those 60 days, at no additional cost.

2.Optional. In the same circumstances, you may instead buy an optional Extended Reporting Period of 12 months, for 75% of the annual premium, or 24 months, for 125%, by asking in writing and paying within 60 days after the Policy Period ends. It starts when the Policy Period ends and replaces the automatic period. The premium is fully earned when paid.

3.Any Extended Reporting Period applies only to Claims for Wrongful Acts committed before the Policy Period ends. It does not increase or reinstate any limit, and it does not extend the first-party coverages.

E. Nonrenewal

If we decide not to renew this policy, or to renew it with a premium increase or reduced coverage, we will give you written notice at least 60 days before the Policy Period ends, or longer if state law requires.

F. Change in control

If, during the Policy Period, the Named Insured merges into or is acquired by another organization, or any person or group acquires more than 50% of its voting rights, this policy will continue until the end of the Policy Period, but only for Incidents Discovered, and Wrongful Acts committed, before the change. You must notify us within 30 days after the change. You may ask us to continue full coverage, and we may agree subject to additional terms and premium.

G. New Subsidiaries and acquisitions

If the Named Insured creates or acquires an organization during the Policy Period whose annual revenue is less than 20% of the Named Insured's, that organization is automatically a Subsidiary. If its revenue is 20% or more, it is a Subsidiary for 90 days after the acquisition, and after that only if we agree in writing. Coverage for a new Subsidiary applies only to Incidents and Wrongful Acts that happen after its creation or acquisition.

H. Territory

This policy applies to Incidents occurring, and Claims brought, anywhere in the world, where legally permissible and subject to Section VI.K.

I. Other insurance

This policy is excess over any other valid insurance that applies to the same Loss, unless that insurance is written specifically as excess of this policy. Incident Response Services are always provided on a primary basis.

J. Subrogation and recoveries

If we pay Loss, we take over your rights of recovery against others to the extent of our payment, and you must help us enforce them. We waive this right against any person or organization you agreed in writing, before the Incident, not to pursue. Any recovery will be applied, after deducting the costs of obtaining it, first to repay you for Loss above the limits, then to repay us, and then to repay you for the Retention and any coinsurance.

K. Sanctions

We will not provide coverage, pay any Loss or provide any benefit to the extent that doing so would expose us to any sanction, prohibition or restriction under the laws or regulations of the United States (including those administered by the Office of Foreign Assets Control), the United Nations, the European Union or the United Kingdom.

L. Action against us

No one may bring legal action against us under this policy unless they have complied with its terms. For the liability coverages, no one may sue us to recover Damages until the Insured's obligation to pay has been fixed by a final judgment or by a written settlement agreement that we consented to. No one has a right under this policy to join us as a party to a Claim against an Insured.

M. Assignment

You may not assign your rights under this policy without our written consent.

N. Bankruptcy

The bankruptcy or insolvency of any Insured does not relieve us of our obligations under this policy.

O. Resolving disagreements

If you and we disagree about this policy, either may ask for non-binding mediation with a mutually agreed mediator, whose fees we will share equally. If the disagreement is not resolved within 60 days after mediation is requested, either may bring an action in a court of competent jurisdiction. This does not limit the appraisal process in Section V.J.

P. Choice of law

This policy is governed by the law of the state shown in Item 11 of the Declarations, except where another state's law must apply. The most-favorable-law provisions in Definitions 14 and 51 apply regardless of this condition.

Q. Conformity to law

Any term of this policy that conflicts with the law of the state in Item 11 is amended to conform to the minimum requirements of that law.

R. Automatic improvements

If, during the Policy Period, we file a revision of this policy form that broadens coverage without additional premium, the broader coverage will apply to this policy from the date the revision takes effect in your state.

S. Currency

All amounts are in U.S. dollars. Loss incurred in another currency is converted at the exchange rate published by the Wall Street Journal on the date the Loss is paid or the judgment or settlement is entered.

T. Risk management services

We may offer optional services, such as external security scans, security alerts and training. Using them is voluntary. Your decision not to use them will not affect your coverage, and our scans are not part of your Application.

U. Headings and summaries

Headings, the “In short” summaries and the plain-English descriptions in the Declarations are for convenience only. They do not form part of the terms of this policy and do not affect its meaning.

V. Entire agreement and changes

This policy (the Declarations, this form, the Application, and the endorsements and disclosures listed in Item 12 of the Declarations) is the entire agreement between you and us. It can be changed only by an endorsement that we issue.
