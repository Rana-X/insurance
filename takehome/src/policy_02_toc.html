How to find what you need

What is covered?Section I, Coverages A–R. The letters match Item 6 of the Declarations.
What do the bold words mean?Section II, in alphabetical order.
What is not covered?Section III. Part A applies to every coverage; Part B to specific coverages.
How much will Harborline pay, and what do I pay?Section IV, together with Items 4–7 of the Declarations.
What do I do when something happens?Section V, and the one-page guide after the Declarations.
What other rules apply?Section VI (cancellation, extended reporting, sanctions and more).
