# 09. Search for 2026 cyber policy wordings (September 27, 2026)

Question: is there a 2026 edition, or a 2026 draft, of a small-business cyber policy to benchmark against, instead of At-Bay's 2023 form?

**Answer: no full 2026 US small-business wording was found.** The closest are:
- a 2026 *draft* standard wording for UK small businesses from the Lloyd's Market Association (LMA), with a US version planned;
- Coalition's 2026 UK specimen;
- new 2026 endorsements (Beazley);
- 2026–27 policies bought by US public bodies, whose council and school-board papers may include quotes or form lists.

Every document host was blocked from this environment (Coalition's UK specimen host, the City of Novi, At-Bay, and all 12 of the 2025–26 US and UK wordings in `catalog/`), so everything below is search-result level.

## Market news found along the way

**Munich Re agreed to acquire At-Bay** for an enterprise value of $575M (announced Aug 19, 2026; closing expected Q1 2027). HSB will oversee the business. At-Bay is described as a top-10 US cyber insurer with $278M of gross written premium, focused on small and mid-sized businesses. The rationale leans on At-Bay as a benchmark, so this is worth knowing for the interview.
