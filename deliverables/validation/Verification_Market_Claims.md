**Top fixes, in priority order**

1. **Coalition's $116K is not an under-$25M figure (#24, Wrong).** $116K is Coalition's average across all policyholders in 2025. For its smallest businesses Coalition reports $77K. The Part 3 sentence misattributes the revenue band.
2. **"Paid-claim frequency" (#45, Partly).** Coalition's 1.21% is a *claims* frequency for businesses under $25M, not a paid-claim frequency. "Higher for firms holding tax data" is the author's assumption, not Coalition data.
3. **Coalition's $0 retention is not tied to MDR (#53, Partly).** Coalition's $0 retention applies when the insured uses Coalition's own incident response team. Its MDR benefits are premium credits and, since 2026, a shorter waiting period. Only At-Bay ties $0 retentions to MDR packages.
4. **"Privacy claims doubled" (#13, Partly).** The figure is real, but it comes from a July 2026 Coalition blog about privacy-rights claims (mostly website-tracking/CIPA suits), not from the claims report listed in Sources. It sits in the data-breach row, where it doesn't belong.
5. **CCH and Kronos were cyberattacks (#20, Partly).** CCH was malware and Kronos was ransomware, so both fall under Coverage E (attacks on vendors). They are not the non-malicious outages that optional Coverage P targets, yet the P sublimit row leans on them.
6. **"Vendors caused 14%" (#16, Partly).** The At-Bay figure is vendors *and customers*. Two of the three mentions drop "and customers".
7. **Lloyd's Y5381 does not "lack" attribution (#40, Partly).** Y5381 requires the clause to set out a robust basis for attribution. What Harborline adds is a defined process and the burden of proof, not attribution itself.
8. **"Checked at the level noted there" (#1, Wrong).** The Sources list gives no check levels.
9. **IRS 7-year record guidance (#65, Partly).** The IRS general period is 3 years. Seven years applies only to specific cases.
10. **At-Bay figures that exist only in the PDF (#10, #15, #25, #55).** The public summaries don't carry $180K, $208K, 14%/$145K or the 80% settlement clause. Cite them to a figure or page in the report or form.

Also missing from Sources (Section 3): the Coalition privacy blog, the AWS post-event summary, Beazley's and CFC's AI announcements, the ISO endorsements, the LMA draft, CFC's client-account cover, and Coalition's and At-Bay's $0-retention programs. The At-Bay report entry also has no URL.
